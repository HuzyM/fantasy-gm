# Fantasy GM

Fantasy roster planning from reported affiliations and observed team schedules.

## Language

**Reported affiliation**:
The professional team reported for a rostered player. It may be incomplete or stale and does not establish the player's NHL identity.
_Avoid_: Confirmed current team

**Observed team fixture**:
A team game present in a loaded schedule observation, with its reported state. It does not confirm that any rostered player will appear.
_Avoid_: Player appearance, guaranteed start

**Weekly window**:
Seven consecutive, inclusive calendar dates in America/Toronto. A weekly window does not imply a Yahoo matchup week.
_Avoid_: Matchup week

**Observed scheduled fixture count**:
The number of distinct eligible team fixtures recorded as scheduled within a weekly window, regardless of whether their recorded start time has passed. This is a count of loaded observations, not a complete schedule or a player-appearance count.
_Avoid_: Games remaining, player starts

**Back-to-back opportunity**:
A team's eligible scheduled fixtures on two consecutive Toronto calendar dates, both inside the selected weekly window. Each adjacent date pair counts once; distinct eligible fixtures for the same team on one date are anomalous under normal regular-season scheduling and do not multiply opportunities or confirm player participation.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed back-to-back starts
