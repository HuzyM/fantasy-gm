# Fantasy GM

Fantasy roster planning from reported affiliations and observed team schedules.

## Language

**Reported affiliation**:
The professional team reported for a rostered player. It may be incomplete or stale and does not establish the player's NHL identity.
_Avoid_: Confirmed current team

**Observed team game**:
A team game present in a loaded schedule observation, with its reported state. It does not confirm that any rostered player will appear.
_Avoid_: Team fixture, player appearance, guaranteed start

**Weekly window**:
Seven consecutive, inclusive calendar dates in America/Toronto. A weekly window does not imply a Yahoo matchup week.
_Avoid_: Matchup week

**Observed scheduled game count**:
The number of distinct eligible team games recorded as scheduled within a weekly window, regardless of whether their recorded start time has passed. This is a count of loaded observations, not a complete schedule or a player-appearance count.
_Avoid_: Fixture count, games remaining, player starts

**Back-to-back opportunity**:
A team's eligible scheduled games on two consecutive Toronto calendar dates, both inside the selected weekly window. Each adjacent date pair counts once; distinct eligible games for the same team on one date are anomalous under normal regular-season scheduling and do not multiply opportunities or confirm player participation.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed back-to-back starts

**Shared game date**:
A Toronto calendar date within the selected weekly window on which both compared players' reported teams have at least one eligible scheduled game observed. Each date counts once, including when the players share a reported team or their teams play each other; this does not establish player appearances or complete schedule coverage.
_Avoid_: Shared player start, guaranteed overlap
